Vesperwing Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Vesperwing (the "app") is a game published by Deluxe Software Solutions SRL, Romania ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. You do not need an account to play, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number inside the app.

What stays on your device

Your progress (the farthest you have flown, feathers, upgrades, flock looks, the story you have seen, your daily-flight and letter history) and your settings are stored only on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are removed. We do not collect them.

Information collected by our service providers

With your consent where the law requires it, the app uses the following services. Each receives technical information from your device, such as an advertising identifier, your approximate location derived from your IP address, device model, operating system version, language and how the ads and the app are used.

The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own. We do not sell your personal information. We do not receive your payment card details; Google Play handles payment.

Consent and your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app asks for your consent the first time it needs to show an ad. No ad is requested until you have answered, and ads are not personalised unless you consent.
  • You can change or withdraw your choice later where the app offers a privacy option for your region.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Watching a rewarded ad is always optional. Buying "Remove ads" stops the ads shown between flights.

Legal basis (EEA and UK)

Consent for ads and their personalisation. Performance of a contract for purchases you make. Our legitimate interest in keeping the app working.

Retention

Advertising data is kept by Google for the periods set in its own policies. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to restore your purchases and meet legal obligations.

International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards they publish (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

Children

Vesperwing is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help remove the data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your data-protection authority (in Romania, ANSPDCP). Because we do not hold identifying details about you, we may need the advertising identifier from your device to find your data. Contact us using the details below.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
